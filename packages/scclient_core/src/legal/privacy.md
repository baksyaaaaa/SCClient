## SC Client Privacy Policy

**Your Microsoft account**
Signing in happens with Microsoft directly. Your sign-in is stored on your own PC and only sent to Microsoft, Xbox and Mojang to start the game and check that you own Minecraft.

**The SC Client service**
Cosmetics, friends and messages go through the SC Client service (cosmetics-md7h.onrender.com, hosted on Render). It keeps:
- your Minecraft username and UUID,
- the cosmetics you picked, so other players can see them,
- your friends list and friend requests,
- your direct messages (the last 200 with each friend).

This is saved in a private GitHub repository so it survives restarts. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: whoever runs the service can technically read them. Removing a friend deletes your messages with them.

**Downloads**
SC Client downloads the game, mods and other files from Mojang, Microsoft, Fabric, Modrinth, CurseForge, Polyfrost's data servers and GitHub. Like any website, those services see your IP address when you download from them.

**No tracking**
SC Client has no ads, no analytics and no usage tracking, and it does not send crash reports to anyone.

**Questions or removing your data**
Open an issue at github.com/baksyaaaaa/SCClient.
